Why two archetypes? A glyph whose ANIM=0 and ANIM=100 outlines differ is a morph: driving 0→100 walks it to the other shape. A glyph that returns to its start at 100 is a pulse: its variation regions peak in the middle of the axis, so 0→100 bulges and comes back. Both use the same axis; only the region geometry differs.
